# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Rust API. React Router (framework mode, SPA) with React Aria Components and Tailwind CSS in `web/`. PostgreSQL is the only database; background jobs use the same database.

## Users

Teams organizing their work, discussing issues, and checking what needs attention.

## Product purpose

Blackwall puts ownership, team plans, issue discussion, and notifications in one workspace.

## Capabilities and constraints

Workspaces, roles, invitations, teams, issues, projects, milestones, workflows, boards, sprints, saved views, comments, notifications, time tracking, reporting, search, member profiles, bulk changes, recovery, import/export, integrations, and account preferences belong in the product.

English and Polish, plus light, dark, and system themes, are required. Browser verification is outside the requested work.

## Brand commitments

The product is named Blackwall. Use direct language and familiar work-management terms.

## Open decisions

The brief does not specify a company identity, logo, deployment host, or native integration provider. Do not invent them.
